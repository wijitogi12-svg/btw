Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: 2026-08-08

1. Labelling

Any paid placement on this site is clearly marked with an Advertisement or Sponsored label so that readers can always distinguish commercial material from editorial content. If a page carries no such label, it is editorial content produced under our Editorial Policy.

2. Separation from editorial

Advertisers have no influence over editorial coverage. Paying for placement does not buy favourable articles, the removal of critical information, or any change to editorial content.

3. What we do not accept

  • Advertising that promises guaranteed profits or specific investment returns.
  • Advertising presented in a way that could be mistaken for unlabelled editorial content.
  • Advertising for services we believe to be fraudulent or unlawful.

4. Affiliate links

We currently do not use affiliate links. If that changes, affected links will be disclosed on the pages where they appear.

5. Questions

Questions about this disclosure can be sent to editorial@sonicobserver.news.
