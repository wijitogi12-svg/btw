Advertising Disclosure

How we label commercial content and keep it separate from our editorial work.

Labelling of Paid Content

  • Any placement paid for by a third party is clearly marked “Advertisement” or “Sponsored” directly on or adjacent to the placement.
  • Sponsored articles, if published, carry the label at the top of the page before the content begins.
  • Affiliate links, if used, are disclosed on the page where they appear.

Editorial Independence

Advertisers have no influence over our editorial coverage. They cannot review, approve or veto editorial articles before publication, and paying for an advertisement does not result in favourable editorial treatment.

What We Do Not Accept

  • Advertisements promising guaranteed profits, fixed returns or risk-free investments.
  • Promotions urging users to invest urgently or presenting digital assets as a certain way to make money.
  • Content that conceals its commercial nature.

Questions

If you are unsure whether a piece of content on this site is commercial, contact us at info@bitway.ae and we will clarify.
