Editorial Policy

1. Source hierarchy

We prioritize primary sources: official announcements, filings, protocol repositories, and regulator publications. Secondary commentary is used for context and always identified as such.

2. Verification and updates

Before publishing, we cross-check factual claims against at least one independent source when possible. Material corrections are reflected in the “Updated” field. If information remains uncertain, we state the uncertainty explicitly.

3. Neutral language standard

We avoid hype framing, guaranteed-return claims, and promotional calls to action. Articles are written to inform, not to persuade users to buy, sell, or hold any asset.

4. Conflicts and transparency

Any paid placement is labeled and governed by our advertising disclosure. Advertising relationships do not determine editorial conclusions.
