Editorial Policy

How we choose topics, source claims, handle corrections and separate editorial content from commercial content.

Contact the desk

Scope and purpose

This policy governs everything published on Bitway Brief. It describes how topics are selected, how claims are verified, and how we behave when we get something wrong. It applies to explainers, analysis, reference pages and any future formats.

Sourcing and verification

Factual claims must be traceable to a source a reader can check. We prioritise primary material — project documentation, published source code, on-chain data and audit reports — and use secondary coverage for corroboration. A claim supported by a single source is attributed in the text so that readers can weigh it themselves.

Where a figure is a configurable parameter that an operator can change, we say so rather than presenting it as fixed. Where we could not verify something, we state that explicitly instead of omitting the uncertainty.

Originality

We do not reproduce third-party articles, documentation or marketing copy. Every page is written from research notes in our own words, with quotation reserved for cases where precise wording is itself the subject. Links point to originals so readers can consult them directly.

Independence and conflicts of interest

No advertiser, sponsor or covered project has any influence over editorial decisions. We do not accept payment to publish, amend or remove coverage. We do not use affiliate or referral links in editorial content. Contributors must disclose any holding or relationship relevant to a topic; where a conflict cannot be managed, the contributor does not work on the piece.

Corrections

Errors are corrected as soon as they are confirmed. Substantive corrections — anything that changes the meaning of a passage — are made in the text and the page's updated date is changed. Minor typographical fixes are made without a note. Readers can request a correction through the contact form; requests that identify the passage and provide a checkable source are handled fastest.

Tooling

Research and drafting tools, including automated ones, may be used to gather material and prepare drafts. Responsibility for accuracy remains with the editorial desk: nothing is published without human review against the underlying sources.

What we do not publish

We do not publish price predictions, trading signals, guaranteed-return claims, or any framing that presents a digital asset as a safe or certain source of income. We do not publish content designed primarily to drive sign-ups to a product.

Disclaimer: this material is for informational purposes only, is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any digital asset.
