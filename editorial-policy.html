Editorial Policy

The rules that govern everything we publish.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy applies to all informational and news content published on bitway.ae. Its purpose is to ensure that readers receive accurate, clearly sourced and neutral information about the technology and digital-asset industry.

2. Sourcing

  • Every article identifies the sources of its key facts with links in a dedicated “Sources” block.
  • We rely on primary sources (official announcements, regulatory filings, project documentation) whenever they are available.
  • We do not copy source articles verbatim. All published texts are original rewrites with added context and analysis.

3. Neutrality

  • We do not publish price predictions, profit promises or “guaranteed return” claims.
  • We do not urge readers to buy, sell or hold any token, coin or financial product.
  • Risks and open questions are described alongside opportunities.

4. Dates, Authorship and Updates

  • Each article displays its publication date, its last update date, and attribution to the Bitway editorial team.
  • Articles are reviewed and updated when the underlying facts change; the “Updated” date reflects the most recent revision.

5. Corrections

Material errors are corrected as soon as they are verified. Readers can report inaccuracies via the contact form or by email at info@bitway.ae.

6. Separation from Advertising

Editorial content is produced independently of any commercial relationships. Paid placements are always labelled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored” — see our Advertising Disclosure.
