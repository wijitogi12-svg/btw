Editorial Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-08

1. Sourcing

Our articles are based on primary sources, above all the official Sonic Labs website and documentation (soniclabs.com, docs.soniclabs.com). Every article lists the sources it relies on. Claims that cannot be traced to a verifiable source are either attributed clearly as opinion or omitted.

2. Originality

We do not reproduce source material verbatim. Each article is an original editorial rewrite that summarises, contextualises and analyses the underlying information in our own words, with links to the originals for readers who want the full detail.

3. Neutrality

We do not publish price targets, profit promises, guaranteed-return claims or calls to buy or sell any asset. Coverage of risks receives the same care as coverage of features. Our writers do not accept payment from projects for favourable coverage.

4. Transparency of dates and authorship

Every article displays its publication date, last-updated date and author attribution. When material facts change, the article is revised and the update date changed accordingly.

5. Corrections

We correct factual errors as quickly as possible. Corrections can be requested via the contact form or by email at editorial@sonicobserver.news. Significant corrections are noted in the affected article.

6. Independence from advertising

Editorial decisions are made independently of any commercial relationships. Any sponsored content is labelled — see the Advertising Disclosure.
