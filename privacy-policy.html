Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-08

1. Who we are

Sonic Observer is an online publication covering the Sonic ecosystem. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the site and how we handle it. Contact for privacy matters: editorial@sonicobserver.news.

2. Data we process

  • Contact form data: the name, email address and message you submit, used solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, requested pages) kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not require registration, do not build marketing profiles and do not sell personal data to anyone.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We process contact data to answer your messages (legitimate interest / your request), and technical data to keep the site secure and functioning (legitimate interest).

4. Retention

Contact correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any reasonable follow-up. Server logs are rotated on a short cycle.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the technical service providers that host this site, and only to the extent needed to operate it. We do not pass your data to advertisers.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, email editorial@sonicobserver.news.

7. Cookies

Details of the cookies used by this site are described in the separate Cookie Policy.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page reflects the latest revision.
