Privacy Policy

What data this site processes and how it is handled.

Summary

BTW News is a static informational website. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what limited data may be processed when you visit the site or contact us.

Data we process

  • Contact form / email: if you contact us, we receive the name, email address, and message you choose to send. This data is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties or used for marketing.
  • Server logs: the hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and requested pages for security and operational purposes.
  • Cookies: see our Cookie Policy. The site itself does not set tracking cookies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not run behavioral advertising profiles.
  • We do not require registration or accounts to read our content.

Third-party links

Articles link to external sources such as project documentation and market trackers. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Review their policies before providing them any data.

Retention and your rights

Correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your inquiry. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact editorial@btwnews.example.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Last updated: 2026-08-08.
