Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-08

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They can be used for essential functionality, preferences, statistics or advertising.

2. Cookies used by this site

This site is intentionally minimal in its use of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary: where the hosting infrastructure sets technical cookies required to serve the site securely, these are used only for that purpose.
  • Analytics and advertising: we currently set no analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated and consent requested where legally required.

3. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings — all major browsers let you block cookies entirely, delete existing ones or receive a warning before a cookie is stored. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect basic site functionality.

4. Changes

Any change to our use of cookies will be reflected on this page, with the revision date updated accordingly.
