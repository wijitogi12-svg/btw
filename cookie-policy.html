Cookie Policy

Which cookies this site uses, which it does not, and how to control storage in your browser.

Contact the desk

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store. Related technologies — local storage, session storage and pixels — perform similar functions. They can be strictly necessary for a site to work, or used for preferences, measurement or advertising.

What this site uses

Bitway Brief is a static publication. It does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and it does not require cookies to display any page. Interface behaviour such as the navigation menu and accordions is handled in memory and is not persisted.

Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies or equivalent mechanisms for security, load balancing and abuse prevention. These are not used to profile readers.

Third-party content

If an article ever embeds third-party content, or if a clearly labelled advertising placement is served by a third party, that provider may set its own cookies under its own policy. Where this happens, the placement is labelled and this page will be updated to describe the provider and purpose before the content is served.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and most browsers offer a private mode that discards storage at the end of a session. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the availability of some hosting-level protections, but it will not prevent you from reading this site.

Updates

If our use of cookies changes, this page will be revised before the change takes effect and the date at the top of the site's legal pages will be updated.
