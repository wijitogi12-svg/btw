FAQ

About this publication

How we work, what we publish and what we deliberately avoid.

Do you give investment advice?

No. Bitway Brief publishes explanatory and analytical material only. Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and no article should be read as a recommendation to buy, sell or hold an asset.

Where does your information come from?

Primary project documentation, public code repositories, block explorers and audit reports, supported by reputable secondary coverage. Every article lists its sources and marks anything that could not be verified.

How often are articles updated?

Articles carry a publication date and an updated date. When a documented parameter changes or an error is found, we revise the piece and update the second date.

Are you affiliated with the Bitway project?

No. We cover the ecosystem as an independent publication and have no commercial relationship with it. Paid placements, if any, are labelled as advertising and never influence coverage.

Do you use referral links?

No. We do not publish referral codes or affiliate links inside editorial content.

How do I report an error?

Use the contact form or write to the editorial desk. Please include the page, the passage in question and a source we can check.
