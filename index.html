Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about this site and its coverage of BTW.

What is Bitway (BTW)?

Bitway (BTW) is a cryptocurrency token described by public sources — including the project's official documentation, a Bitrue blog article, and a Coinranking listing — as a utility token for the Bitway ecosystem. Our articles summarize what these sources say and note which claims remain unverified.

Is BTW News affiliated with the Bitway project?

No. BTW News is an independent editorial project. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or compensated by the Bitway project, any exchange, or any market participant.

Where does your information come from?

Articles are based on publicly available sources: the official Bitway documentation, market trackers such as Coinranking, and exchange publications such as the Bitrue blog. Every article lists its sources with links.

Do you provide investment advice?

No. Nothing on this site is investment, financial, trading, legal, or tax advice. All content is informational and educational only. Please read our Risk Disclaimer before acting on any information.

How do you handle corrections?

When an error is identified, we correct the article directly and refresh its “Updated” date. You can report errors via the Contact page — correction reports are treated as a priority.

How is sponsored content labeled?

Any paid placement is clearly marked as “Advertisement” or “Sponsored”. Unlabeled content is always independent editorial work. Details are in our Advertising Disclosure.

Can I republish your articles?

You may quote brief excerpts with attribution and a link back to the original article. Full republication requires permission — reach out via the Contact page.
