FAQ

Common Questions

Short factual answers, with links to primary sources where relevant.

What is Sonic?

Sonic is an EVM-compatible layer-1 blockchain developed by Sonic Labs, designed for high throughput and sub-second transaction finality. Details are published at docs.soniclabs.com.

What is the S token used for?

According to the official token page, S is the network's native token used to pay transaction fees, and for staking and validator operation. See soniclabs.com/token for the primary source.

Is Sonic Observer affiliated with Sonic Labs?

No. We are an independent editorial project. We summarise and analyse publicly available information and always link to the official sources we used.
