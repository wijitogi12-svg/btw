About

Bitway Crypto News is an informational newsroom focused on digital-asset infrastructure, market structure, and policy developments. Our mission is to help readers understand what changed, why it matters, and which risks remain open. We prioritize accuracy, plain language, and clear sourcing over speed alone.

Our editorial process starts with publicly available primary documents such as regulator statements, exchange filings, protocol announcements, and issuer reports. Writers summarize the core facts, then provide context about potential impact on liquidity, operations, or user protections. Each article includes a publication date, update log, source links, and a disclaimer that coverage is not financial advice.

We revise content when official numbers are corrected, when policy language is clarified, or when protocol timelines shift. Corrections are reflected in the “Updated” field to preserve traceability. We avoid promotional claims and do not publish guaranteed-return narratives. This approach supports readers who need decision support inputs without marketing bias.
