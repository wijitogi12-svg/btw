Our mission

Bitway Brief exists to make on-chain finance infrastructure legible to people who are not developers. The digital-asset sector produces an enormous amount of promotional material and very little plain description. We publish the plain description: what a product does, how it is documented to work, what is verifiable, and what is not.

We are an independent publication. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or acting on behalf of any project we cover, including the Bitway ecosystem that gives this publication its subject matter. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage, we do not publish referral links, and we do not run price predictions.

How our content is produced

Every explainer starts from primary material: project documentation, public source repositories, block explorers, and published audit reports. Secondary coverage is used to corroborate, never as a sole source for a factual claim. Where a claim rests on a single source, we attribute it in the text rather than presenting it as settled.

Articles are written from scratch. We do not reproduce third-party text; where a formulation matters, we describe it and link to the original. Each page lists the sources used, the date of publication and the date of the most recent substantive update.

Accuracy and corrections

Parameters in this sector change frequently — fees, queue times, supported networks, reward rates. We therefore describe mechanisms rather than presenting numbers as permanent, and we mark configurable values as configurable. When a documented parameter changes, we revise the affected article and update its date.

If you find an error, tell us. Substantive corrections are made in the text and noted; typographical fixes are made silently. We treat a correction as a routine part of publishing, not an exception.

What we will not publish

We do not publish guaranteed-return claims, profit projections, trading signals, or calls to acquire any token. We do not publish content that presents an incentive programme as risk-free income. If a topic cannot be covered without those framings, we do not cover it.
