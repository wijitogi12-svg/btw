BitChat AI

BitChat AI is an informational assistant that summarizes public crypto-related documents, organizes glossary definitions, and helps users compare policy language across releases.

It is designed for research support and does not execute trades, custody funds, or provide individualized recommendations.

Core functions include source-linked summaries, chronology views for evolving stories, and side-by-side comparisons of policy drafts.
