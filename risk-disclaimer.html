Market and product risk

Digital assets are volatile and can lose most or all of their value in short periods. Yield-bearing products carry additional layers of risk, including smart-contract defects, oracle failure, counterparty and custody failure, liquidity constraints, redemption queues and the possibility that withdrawals are suspended. Staking positions may be subject to lock-up periods and to penalties for validator misbehaviour.

Past performance, historical rates and advertised returns are not indicators of future results. Rewards paid in a project's own token depend on that token's market value, which may fall.
